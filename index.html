Shangarapillai

photographer · since 2018

With a gentle heart and an eye for the candid, Shangarapillai captures the fleeting beauty of childhood and family bonds. Based in Rümlang, Zürich, his work is defined by bright, airy tones and authentic emotions — inspired by the innocence of babies and the laughter of families. Every session is a quiet storytelling, creating heirlooms that feel like warm sunlight.

Founder of @kethees_sambu and the dedicated baby portfolio @pearlbaby_photography, Shangarapillai’s style blends documentary immediacy with a dreamy, fine‑art finish.
